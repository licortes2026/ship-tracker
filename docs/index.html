Type the noon position and it joins the magenta track. Kept on this device where storage allows.

Port itinerary

    This chart draws itself, with no map tiles and no outside requests, so it works in the app and on a weak ship connection. The solid ink line is the route advanced by the schedule, which is dead reckoning rather than a fix. Magenta is real positions.

    When served from the track server, real AIS positions arrive on their own and draw in magenta. Live AIS elsewhere needs a browser tab of its own, since those sites refuse to be embedded.
